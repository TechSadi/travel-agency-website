A Vadodara travel desk since 2009

Suman Holidays started as a two-desk office in Fatehgunj booking train tickets and Char Dham yatras. Today a team of twelve plans family holidays, honeymoons and group tours to more than 40 destinations.

What hasn’t changed: you can still walk in, sit down with the person planning your trip, and call them directly while you’re away.
